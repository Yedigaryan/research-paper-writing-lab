READER SUMMARY
The paper asks three questions about a small graph given by its adjacency matrix A: what the entries of the powers A^k count, what a path between two vertices is, and how to tell from A alone whether the graph is connected. Its answers are that (A^k)_ij counts walks of length k from vertex i to vertex j (repeated vertices allowed, so not paths); a path is a walk with no repeated vertex; and a graph on N vertices is connected exactly when every off-diagonal entry of A + A^2 + ... + A^(N-1) is positive. The connectivity test is justified by showing that any walk contains a path of length at most N - 1, and the paper checks the test on a three-vertex connected graph and a three-vertex disconnected graph. I could write this paragraph without consulting any source, though the reasons for the N - 1 cutoff and for the k = 0 convention were only inferable from the proofs.
