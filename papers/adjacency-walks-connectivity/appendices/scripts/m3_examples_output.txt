Why the exponent N-1 is needed and the sum (or I+A) is needed
connected example, A alone: (A)_13 = 0 so exponent 1 misses the pair n1,n3
A^2 alone: (A^2)_12 = 0 so A^(N-1) alone misses the pair n1,n2 (parity)
diagonal of A^2 = [1, 2, 1] = degrees [1, 2, 1]
