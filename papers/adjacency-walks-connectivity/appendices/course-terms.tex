\section{Textbook locators and the board's notation}\label{app:courseterms}

\Cref{tab:locators} lists, in the textbook's build order, where each concept used in this note is defined in the course textbook \cite{petrosyan2015grafneri}.
Page numbers are the printed ones; the PDF page is one higher.
Only the pages named in the table, and the pages that carry the examples cited in the text, were read page by page; the formulas were checked on the page images.
